| Product | Free | Paid | What the paid tier adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| ProjectionLab | Forecasts, Monte Carlo, backtesting | US$129/yr | What-if scenarios, compare mode, multiple plans, downloadable reports, tax and withdrawal strategy |

| Our own: PayCheckly, InvoiceKit | — | — | PayCheckly covers take-home pay; Finealth must not lead with salary tools |

## What people pay for
1. Nobody pays for a single formula; calculators are the free SEO funnel.
2. People pay for a plan about their own situation: debt-free date, goal plans, retirement plan, score with a to-do list.
3. Repeated paid features: what-if scenarios and compare mode, saved plans, reports/export, no ads, deeper charts.
4. Bank sync commands US$100+/yr but costs money per user and needs regulated access; our angle is the private, no-login opposite.

## Differentiators
- Health score with ranked, sourced actions (not just a number).
- Private: no account, no bank link, data never leaves the device.
- India-first maths (₹, lakh formatting, EMI/SIP conventions) with US/UK/EU support.
- Price well below every paid competitor (₹199/yr, against ₹499/yr for ET Money premium and US$95-109/yr for Copilot, Monarch and YNAB).

## Scope decisions (non-goals)
Bank linking, product recommendations, budgeting/transaction tracking, cloud sync, shared household accounts.
